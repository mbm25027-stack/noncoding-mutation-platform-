Direct ΔPWM Shift Analysis

Unlike CADD (which relies on evolutionary conservation and misses human-specific promoter mutations), NC-MutScan directly computes transcription factor log-odds binding affinity gains/losses (ΔPWM).

ENCODE 4 cCRE Epigenomic Prior

Integrates live chromatin accessibility data (ATAC-seq / H3K27ac) to filter out false-positive motif matches occurring in inactive or heterochromatic genomic regions.

AID/SHM Somatic Hypermutation Filter

Discriminates true oncogenic driver events from AID-mediated somatic hypermutation (WRCY repetitive motifs) in lymphoma and B-cell malignancies.

Sub-15ms Execution Speed

Optimized multithreaded motif scoring engine executes in under 15ms per locus, avoiding external API latency bottlenecking.

Noncoding Genome Mechanics

Why Noncoding Mutations Matter

Over 98% of the human genome does not encode proteins, yet it harbors hundreds of thousands of active enhancers, promoters, and insulator elements. Mutations in these noncoding regulatory regions drive tumorigenesis by perturbing transcription factor recruitment.

Computational Framework

Integrated Scientific Framework

NC-MutScan evaluates noncoding substitutions by scoring position-weight matrix binding energy shifts (ΔPWM) across JASPAR and HOCOMOCO TF databases combined with ENCODE 4 regulatory element annotations and 3D Hi-C loop anchors.

1 FASTA Input Sequence + Locus
2 ΔPWM + Epigenomic Prior cCRE + Tissue Context
3 3D Loop & Driver Call Hi-C Anchor Target
  • JASPAR 2024 & HOCOMOCO v11 Motifs
    Direct position weight matrix log-odds alignment for over 800 human transcription factor binding profiles.
  • ENCODE Candidate Cis-Regulatory Elements (cCREs)
    Integration with promoter (PLS) and enhancer (pELS / dELS) chromatin accessibility maps.
  • 3D Chromatin Topology (Hi-C / Micro-C Anchors)
    Maps distal enhancer mutations directly to target gene promoter loops rather than assuming nearest linear gene proximity.
  • AID/SHM Somatic Hypermutation Filter
    Discriminates benign somatic hypermutation (WRCY repetitive motifs) in lymphoma from true oncogenic drivers.
